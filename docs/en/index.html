Live in a Hurry

How many minutes are left today?

269min

Not “4 hours 29 minutes.” Just “269 minutes left.”

When we think in hours or days, time feels plentiful. Live in a Hurry shows the time left until the end of your day, or a deadline you choose, in a single unit — minutes or seconds — as a quiet reminder of how precious it is.

Features

  • See the time left until the end of the day in minutes or seconds
  • Set any deadline you like: bedtime, the end of your workday, a due date
  • Tap the number to switch between minutes and seconds
  • Switches to seconds automatically when less than a minute is left
  • Repeat every day, or count down once to a specific date and time
  • Give your timer any title
  • Widgets for the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy
  • Light, Dark, and Navy themes

FAQ

What happens when the deadline passes?
A daily timer automatically starts counting toward the same time the next day. A one-time timer ends.
Can I set a deadline after midnight?
Yes. If you set it to 2:00 AM, at 11:00 PM it shows “180 minutes left,” counting to 2:00 AM the next day.
What about when I travel?
The countdown always follows your iPhone’s time zone.
Where is my data stored?
Your settings are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.
