Live in a Hurry

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 25, 2026

This policy explains how Kazuki Watanabe (“the developer”) handles information in the iOS app Live in a Hurry (Japanese name: 生き急ぎタイマー, “the app”).

1. Information We Collect

The app does not collect any personal information. It never sends your name, email address, location, device identifiers, usage data, or any other information to the developer or to third parties.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • Timer settings (title, deadline time or date, repeat setting)
  • App settings (display unit, keep screen on, theme)

This information is never sent to the developer and is deleted from your device when you delete the app. If you use iOS backups, it may be included in your device backup.

3. Network and Third-Party Services

The app does not connect to the internet and does not use any third-party services or tools for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

4. Children

Because the app collects no information, it collects no information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

If the handling of information changes — for example, if advertising or in-app purchases are added in the future — this page will be updated beforehand. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
